Privacy Policy

Complete disclosure of user data privacy, device permissions, and security practices for the Lakshya Academy Tuition & Coaching Institute application.

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Lakshya Academy (referred to as "we", "our", "us", or "the Application"), operated by PixelFizz. Lakshya Academy is an educational tuition and coaching institute management platform designed for teachers, tutors, and coaching institute administrators to efficiently manage daily attendance, batch scheduling, fee collection, and academic progress.

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy of our teachers, tutors, students, and parents. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal data in accordance with the Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations.

2. Information We Collect

To provide core coaching institute management functionalities, the Application processes the following categories of data:

A. Student & Academic Records

  • Student Identity: Student Name, General Register (GR) Number, Roll Number, Standard/Grade, School Name, and Enrolled Batches.
  • Contact Information: Student phone number, Parent/Guardian phone numbers, and optional emergency contact.
  • Academic Logs: Daily attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, Excused), test marks, syllabus completion notes, and enrollment/exit dates.

B. Tuition Fee & Transaction Records

  • Fee Accounting: Yearly/Monthly fee structure, concessions/discounts, fee installment amounts collected, payment mode (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer), transaction reference notes, and sequential receipt numbers.
  • Payment Processing: We do NOT collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs. Tuition payments are recorded by the teacher for institute bookkeeping and receipt generation only.

C. Educator Account Information

  • Authentication: Teacher name, email address, and institute designation authenticated securely via Google Firebase Authentication.

3. Android Device Permissions & Explicit Disclosures

Google Play requires clear, prominent disclosures regarding sensitive device permissions. Below is the precise reason each permission is requested:

📱 Contacts Permission (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS):
The Application provides a convenience feature allowing teachers to bulk-import student records directly from their address book.

Strict Privacy Guarantee:
• Access to contacts is strictly user-initiated (only triggered when the teacher taps "Import from Contacts").
• Only contacts explicitly selected by the teacher are saved into their tuition batch.
• Your address book is NEVER uploaded in bulk, monitored in background, sold to data brokers, shared with marketing networks, or used for unsolicited communication.

🔔 Notifications Permission (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
Used solely to deliver class session countdowns, live batch start alerts, and scheduled reminder notifications on the educator's device.

4. How We Use Collected Data

The information collected is strictly utilized to operate the educational management suite:

  • To maintain accurate student rosters and batch attendance registers.
  • To generate sequential, official tuition fee PDF receipts and academic reports.
  • To facilitate teacher-initiated WhatsApp updates (e.g., sending payment confirmation receipts or absentee notices directly to parents).
  • To track student enrollment history and institute analytics.

5. Data Storage & Security Measures

All cloud-synchronized data is stored on enterprise-grade Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure utilizing industry-standard security:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and cloud servers is protected using 256-bit TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Tenant Isolation: Data is organized under isolated institute tenant hierarchies, preventing unauthorized cross-access.
  • No Advertising: Lakshya Academy is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers that profile user behavior.

6. Third-Party Integrations

The Application interacts with trusted services necessary for operational reliability:

  • Google Firebase: Backend authentication and secure cloud database (Firebase Privacy Policy).
  • WhatsApp / Device Share: When the teacher chooses to share a PDF receipt or absentee notice, the file is handed over to the device's native sharing sheet or WhatsApp API upon explicit user confirmation.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Lakshya Academy is an institutional administrative tool operated exclusively by adult educators, teachers, and tuition directors. We do not solicit personal information directly from children under 13 without institutional and parental authorization. All student records are entered and managed under the supervision of the educator.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, users have full control over their data:

  • Student Record Deletion: Teachers can delete or mark inactive any student record directly within the app roster.
  • Full Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your entire institute account and all associated student, attendance, and fee records permanently from our cloud database, you may submit a request by emailing support@pixelfizz.com or manish0319@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". All records will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect enhancements in our application or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
